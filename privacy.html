Privacy

ProtoLink Diagnostics is a static, 100% client-side web app.

  • No data leaves your device. Frames, logs and notes you paste or open are processed locally in your browser. There is no server, analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • Local storage only. If you press “Save”, the item is stored in your browser’s IndexedDB (or localStorage as a fallback). DCOM checklist ticks use localStorage. Use Saved → Delete everything or clear site data to remove it.
  • Offline cache. A service worker stores the app files so it works without a connection.
  • Hosting. The static files are served by your host (e.g. GitHub Pages), which may keep standard access logs under its own policy.

Tip: field logs can contain IP addresses, tag names and credentials. Don’t save sensitive logs on shared devices.
